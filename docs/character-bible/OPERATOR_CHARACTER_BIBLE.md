**Locked:** 2026-05-02

## Facial features (anatomy)

- **Eyes:** narrow, intent, dark — visible only above the half-mask. Pupils slightly catch the red sigil glow.
- **Brow:** straight, set. No surprise. No theatrics.
- **Lower face:** completely covered by a matte-black half-mask (nose-bridge to chin). No skin showing below the eyes except a sliver of jawline at the side.
- **Sigil:** the 暗殺 kanji is stamped in arterial red on the forehead plate of the mask. Single glow point, no halo bloom.

## Hair

- Concealed by hood. Not visible at all in standard shots.
- If hood is removed (rare hero moment): clean undercut, dark hair, no styling. Functional, not styled.

## Body type

- Lean, tall (~6 ft / 183 cm), efficient build. Not bodybuilder, not skinny — Wing Chun practitioner physique.
- Posture: still, balanced, weight evenly distributed. Hands relaxed at sides or on terminal keys.
- Movement (when shown): minimal. The work happens; the body barely shifts.

## Art style

- **Photoreal.** Not anime. Not illustrated. Not stock.
- **Cinematic still** — anamorphic 2.39:1 framing, 35mm film grain
- **A24 colour grade** — blacks lifted (not crushed), highlights soft, mid-tones controlled
- **Filmic tone-mapping** — no HDR look, no oversaturated colours
- **Reference films:** *Drive* (2011) restraint, *The Social Network* opening confidence, *Death Stranding* hooded silhouette, Apple iPhone 15 Pro launch lighting, BIG architectural films

## Discipline rules — non-negotiable

1. **Red is sigil-only.** Not on monitors, not on the floor, not in the haze. ONE point of saturated red per frame, on the mask.
2. **Lighting is white architectural studio.** No warm orange, no candlelight, no neon, no cyan, no green, no purple.
3. **Photoreal, not illustrated.** If a render comes back as anime / vector / stylised, re-prompt.
4. **Half-mask + hood always present** in standard shots. Hood-off mask reveal is a single hero-moment shot only.
5. **Eyes only visible above the mask.** No full-face shots.
6. **Composition has architectural depth.** Vanishing-point perspective, gallery proportions, controlled negative space. NOT a SOC dungeon.

## What this character does (for prompt context)

The operator runs a full SaaS acquisition system from one terminal — lead-gen, outreach, demos, calendar, pricing, objections, copy, onboarding, contracts, follow-up. While the founder sleeps, the operator works. While the founder refreshes their inbox, the operator dispatches ten manual tasks like a Wing Chun master dispatches ten challengers. **One operator. Every system. Always running.**
